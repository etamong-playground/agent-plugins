# Audience review

Resolve the reader before choosing content, including when the requester and reader are different people.
Apply this discipline while drafting, then review the rendered result from that reader's perspective.

## Establish the audience

From the request and destination, identify who will read it, what action or decision they need to take, what they already know, and what access and tools they have.
Reuse an audience already established for the repository or deliverable.
Keep this working brief out of the artifact unless the reader needs a scope statement.
Ask only when a missing audience fact changes the content materially; otherwise state a reasonable assumption in conversation and proceed.

## Draft for the reader's task

Organize the artifact around what the reader must understand, choose, do, and verify.
Explain unfamiliar components on first use, put prerequisites before actions, and provide examples with expected results when the reader is meant to act.
Follow the repository's language and writing conventions; use available Korean prose guidance for Korean artifacts.

Keep authoring history out unless it changes the reader's action.
Do not publish working notes or a description of the writing process unless the reader needs them to act.
A true sentence still needs a reason to be present.
Put useful source and license details in a compact reference section.
Preserve required attribution, disclosures, material limits, and failure recovery.

## Review before delivery

Read without relying on the conversation.
For each section, identify the reader's question it answers and the action or understanding it enables.
Remove or relocate material that only reassures the author that work was performed.
Check descriptive headings, environment-appropriate examples, working references, and commands with verifiable results.
Inspect the rendered page when layout or a diagram matters.

Report concrete mismatches with location, reader impact, and a proposed correction.
Do not invent a mismatch to fill a template.
A review-only request leaves the artifact unchanged; otherwise apply corrections within the authorized scope.

## Completion

The reader can complete the stated task using the artifact and its explicit prerequisites, without private conversation context.
Necessary uncertainty remains visible, and irrelevant preparation notes are absent.
